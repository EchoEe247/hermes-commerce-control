# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Hermes Commerce Control welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements on their merits;
- give specific, actionable review feedback;
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption;
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information;
- avoid spam, impersonation, fabricated contribution history, or manipulated project metrics;
- disclose relevant conflicts when they materially affect a contribution or review.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, sexualized or discriminatory conduct, doxxing, sustained off-topic disruption, abusive review behavior, and deliberate attempts to bypass repository security or community rules.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when conduct violates this policy.

For ordinary moderation concerns, use the relevant GitHub reporting mechanism or contact the maintainers through the repository. For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` instead of publishing exploit details in a conduct report.

Maintainers should apply this policy consistently and avoid using moderation to suppress good-faith technical disagreement.
